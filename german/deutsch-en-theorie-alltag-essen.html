Examples and explanations from this topic. Use the patterns to form your own sentences.

Brot

Water: Ich trinke ___.

Wasser
Complete sentence: Ich trinke Wasser.

An apple: Ich esse einen ___.

Apfel
Complete sentence: Ich esse einen Apfel.

Breakfast: Am Morgen esse ich mein ___.

Frühstück
Complete sentence: Am Morgen esse ich mein Frühstück.

Reading strategy

Read for the situation first: who is speaking, where are they, and what do they want? Then find the exact information asked for. Check names, numbers, time expressions and negatives before answering.
