Shopping · Level 1

Examples and explanations from this topic. Use the patterns to form your own sentences.

The price: Wie viel ___ das?

kostet
Complete sentence: Wie viel kostet das?

I buy: Ich ___ einen Pullover.

kaufe
Complete sentence: Ich kaufe einen Pullover.

Too expensive: Das ist zu ___.

teuer
Complete sentence: Das ist zu teuer.

The shop: Ich gehe ins ___.

Geschäft
Complete sentence: Ich gehe ins Geschäft.

Reading strategy

Read for the situation first: who is speaking, where are they, and what do they want? Then find the exact information asked for. Check names, numbers, time expressions and negatives before answering.
