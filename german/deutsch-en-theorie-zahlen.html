📖 Units before tens

From 21 to 99, say the unit first, then und, then the tens. Write the whole number as one word. This order differs from English.

NumberGermanPattern
21einundzwanzigone + and + twenty
24vierundzwanzigfour + and + twenty
35fünfunddreissigfive + and + thirty
99neunundneunzignine + and + ninety

🦊 Eins, ein and eine

Use eins when counting or naming the number by itself. Before a noun, use the appropriate form of ein: ein Buch, eine Tasche, einen Apfel. In compound numbers such as einundzwanzig, use ein, not eins.

eins, zwei, drei · ein Buch · eine Tasche · zwei Bücher

⚠️ Common mistakes

16 is sechzehn, not sechszehn; 17 is siebzehn, not siebenzehn. The same shortened stems appear in sechzig and siebzig. This page uses Swiss spelling: dreissig. In Germany and Austria, write dreißig.

📖 Writing large numbers

Number words below one million are normally written as one word. Million and Milliarde are nouns: capitalise them and write them separately. They take plural endings after two or more.

zweihundertvierunddreissig → 234
dreitausendfünfhundert → 3 500
eine Million zweihunderttausend → 1 200 000

Usually add -te up to 19 and -ste from 20 onwards. Erste, dritte, siebte and achte need special attention. Ordinal numbers behave like adjectives, so their endings change with gender and case.

der erste Tag → the first day
am ersten Tag → on the first day
die zweite Seite → the second page

🕒 Times

Halb neun means 8:30: halfway to nine. For appointments, use um. Regional quarter-hour expressions vary; Viertel nach and Viertel vor are widely understood.

Es ist halb neun. → It is half past eight.
Der Kurs beginnt um dreizehn Uhr dreissig. → The course starts at 13:30.

📅 Dates, age and phone numbers

Dates use ordinal numbers: der zehnte Oktober, am zehnten Oktober. For age, use sein. Phone numbers can be read digit by digit for clarity.

Heute ist der zehnte Oktober. → Today is 10 October.
Ich bin achtundzwanzig Jahre alt. → I am 28 years old.
079 → null, sieben, neun

Write down your age, a price, the time, a date and a house number. Say each in German and check the relevant pattern above.
