What will I learn?

Level 1 · Foundations

You can introduce yourself, ask simple questions and express wishes.

Level 2 · Everyday confidence

You can describe experiences, explain plans and politely ask for help.

Level 3 · Connected communication

You can justify opinions, explain problems and suggest solutions.

These levels describe our internal difficulty stages. They are not certified A1, A2 or B1 levels.
