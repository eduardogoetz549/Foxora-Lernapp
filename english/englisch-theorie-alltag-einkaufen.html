Lies zuerst die Situation. Achte auf Personen, Zeitangaben und Verneinungen. Beim Schreiben sind die hinterlegten Varianten erlaubt; die Musterlösung ist ein Beispiel.

A comparison site listed a refurbished laptop at a lower price than a new one. The seller provided a one-year warranty but warned that the battery might show signs of use. Before buying, Ethan checked the return policy and asked whether delivery was insured. He decided that the saving justified the risk.

I would like to know whether delivery is insured.
Indirekte Fragen behalten die normale Satzstellung.

If I had the receipt, I could return it.
Second Conditional beschreibt die gedachte Möglichkeit.

The device should have been checked beforehand.
Should have been checked ist ein Modalpassiv zur Vergangenheit.
